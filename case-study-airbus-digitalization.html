The challenge

Good ideas need a path into daily work.

In quality engineering, digital opportunities emerge from operational friction: process handoffs, fragmented information and repetitive work. The challenge is to turn those observations into a coherent transformation agenda with clear priorities and people willing to carry it forward.

My contribution

01 · Understand

Start with the real process

  • Translate engineering and quality needs into requirements, technical assessments and practical solution options.
  • Connect improvement ideas to the user journey and operational context.

02 · Prioritise

Build a structured path from idea to delivery

  • Design and launch an automation and analytics pipeline for assessing and delivering digital quality-improvement projects.
  • Contribute to a management-level roadmap across AI, data analytics and process automation.

03 · Mobilise

Make adoption part of the work

  • Build a community of digital ambassadors to surface and advance improvement ideas.
  • Support coordination through shared collaboration platforms and cross-functional ways of working.

Evidence of scope

AI, analytics and adoption in one agenda.

The wider role includes leading AI deployment and implementation across 20+ use cases, alongside digitalization and quality-improvement work.

These examples describe my responsibilities and delivery scope. The value I bring is the connection between business priorities, technical decisions and the people responsible for implementation.

Why it transfers

A practical approach for complex organisations.

When a team has many ideas and competing priorities, I help make the problem clear, establish a practical sequence and connect delivery with adoption. This is the experience I bring to transformation leadership and advisory conversations.
