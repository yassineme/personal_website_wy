AI strategy that connects to delivery.

Priorities, ownership and adoption for teams turning AI activity into a practical next step.

AI strategy connects a business outcome to the decisions, responsibilities and changes needed to deliver it. A useful roadmap explains what to do next, who owns the work and what evidence will justify scaling.

I’m Yassine W. Medjati, an AI and digital transformation leader based in Hamburg. My articles explore practical questions about priorities, ownership and adoption.

Prioritise AI use cases around an operating problem

When a team has many ideas, the first task is to agree which problem matters. A strategy conversation can clarify the intended outcome, the people affected, the available information and the dependencies that need attention before delivery starts.

A useful starting point is a short description of each initiative: the person, the task, the friction, the owner and the next decision. My five-decision guide to AI roadmap prioritisation provides a working frame for that discussion.

Connect AI implementation with adoption

A successful demonstration is only one part of implementation. The surrounding workflow, user expectations, support and feedback need owners too. Reviewing those together helps a leadership team understand what is ready for a broader rollout and what still needs to be learned.

My AI adoption review sets out practical questions about the whole task, useful expectations, feedback, ongoing support and evidence of usefulness.

Give GenAI an operating model

As experiments multiply, teams need a shared way to decide what continues, what changes and who remains accountable. That includes workflow choices, human review, ongoing maintenance and a rhythm for revisiting decisions.

The GenAI operating model discussion guide offers a starting agenda for leadership. It is a working perspective for discussion, rather than a promise of a particular delivery outcome.

Start with your next decision

For an initial conversation, describe the operating problem, the AI activity already in motion and the decision you need to make next. We can discuss where priorities, delivery or adoption need greater clarity.
