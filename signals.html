How this collection works

Company announcements sit alongside independent reporting, practitioners and policy institutions. We check feeds on arrival and every five minutes while this page is visible. Collection pauses when nobody is visiting; no scheduled collection is enabled.

Related reports are grouped using conservative title matching. Publisher excerpts retain attribution. Topics and business questions are assigned automatically using topic rules; they are not generated article summaries or commentary from Yassine. Follow the source to assess the complete claim.

The archive retains up to 1,500 reports from the last 180 days. “New” compares story groups with your previous browsing session; a session continues for 30 minutes of inactivity. A first visit establishes your baseline. Saved stories and reading history stay in this browser and do not sync between devices.
