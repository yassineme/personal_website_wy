Your data / Your choices

Privacy & cookies.

What this website uses, what stays optional, and how to change your choice.

Owner-review draft · 26 October 2026

This page describes the prepared website setup. Google Analytics and Vercel Web Analytics are configured for production use after visitor opt-in. Controller contact details, provider agreements and the legal basis for the final deployment must be checked before this becomes the final privacy notice. A cookie banner alone does not complete the site’s legal requirements.

Who to contact

Website owner: Yassine W. Medjati. For privacy questions, contact hello@wyassine.com. Complete controller contact information remains to be confirmed for the final notice.

Website delivery and AI Signals

The public website is hosted on Vercel. Delivering a page requires technical connection information such as the IP address, requested URL and browser information. Necessary hosting/security processing is separate from optional audience analytics. The final notice must confirm the hosting entity, processing agreement, log retention and any international-transfer safeguards.

AI Signals requests a feed from this website’s own endpoint. The server retrieves public publisher feeds; your browser is not sent to those publishers just to show the headline stack. Following an original article opens that publisher’s website under its own privacy rules.

Essential storage and preferences

Your cookie choice is stored locally in your browser as ywm-privacy-consent-v2 for 180 days. It records the selected providers, decision date and notice version; it contains no random visitor identifier. The site also remembers preferences you request, including paused AI Signals, dismissed stories and up to 200 saved signals. AI Signals also stores story identifiers and the time of your recent browsing session locally to calculate “new since your last visit.” This history stays in your browser, is not sent to the archive server, and can be cleared on the Signals page. The shared archive contains public publisher material, not visitor reading histories. Local fonts are served by this website.

Optional Vercel Web Analytics

Configured for production use, only after your opt-in. Vercel Web Analytics can process page views and aggregate visit information. This website gates it behind your choice even though the service does not use analytics cookies. URL query strings and fragments are removed from the events sent by this integration.

Read Vercel’s Web Analytics privacy information ↗. The final notice must confirm the contracted provider, relevant retention, transfers and safeguards.

Optional Google Analytics 4

Configured for the production website and activated only after your opt-in. Google Analytics 4 may measure page views, referrers and device/browser information and set first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_…. This integration limits those cookies to 180 days, disables advertising personalization and Google Signals, and strips query strings and fragments from page URLs and referrers. It does not send contact forms, booking details, names or email addresses as analytics events.

The setup uses basic consent mode: no Google Analytics script, measurement requests or consent pings are loaded before opt-in. Google may process data outside the EU. Before activation, confirm the data-processing terms, recipients, transfer mechanism, property-level data retention and required disclosures. See Google’s privacy policy ↗.

AI stock-market ticker

The AI markets ticker requests public-company quotes from this website’s own endpoint. The server retrieves the quotes from Finnhub and temporarily caches them to limit repeated requests. Your browser does not connect directly to Finnhub to display the ticker, and this integration does not forward visitor IP addresses, contact details or booking information to the quote provider. Market-data requests are separate from optional audience analytics.

Finnhub receives the technical information needed for the server’s data requests. Read Finnhub’s terms and privacy information ↗.

Enquiries and CV access requests

When you submit an enquiry or request the CV PIN or PDF, this website sends your name, email address, message and request type through its server to a private Google Sheet. Google Apps Script records the request, sends the website owner an email notification and sends you an automatic acknowledgement. Replies to the acknowledgement go to the owner’s contact mailbox. Your email is used as the reply-to address so the owner can reply personally. The form does not automatically send you a PIN or résumé. The owner manages review status and deletion in the Sheet. The final notice must confirm the Google account/provider arrangement and retention period. Booking appointments continue through Cal.com.

Booking via Cal.com

Cal.com loads only after you choose a booking link, such as “Discuss a role”. Opening the calendar connects your browser to Cal.com and shares technical connection information. If you proceed, Cal.com handles the availability, contact details and booking information you enter. The calendar may use storage needed to provide the requested booking service. This is separate from optional website analytics.

You can close the calendar without booking or contact me by email. See Cal.com’s privacy information ↗. The final notice must confirm the applicable booking-provider arrangement and retention.

Change or withdraw your choice

Use “Cookie settings” in the footer on any page. “Only essential” withdraws both analytics choices in one action. When analytics were running, the page refreshes to unload them; accessible Google Analytics cookies are removed. Previously transmitted data cannot be undone by changing this setting, but you may make a data-protection request. Changed provider configuration or a new notice version requires a fresh choice.

Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and, where relevant, object to processing. You may withdraw consent prospectively without affecting processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal. You can complain to the competent supervisory authority. The final notice must confirm the controller’s establishment, applicable legal bases and supervisory contact.
