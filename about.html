About Yassine W. Medjati

Perspectives on AI strategy and transformation, with people at the centre.

I’m Yassine W. Medjati, an AI and digital transformation leader based in Hamburg, Germany. My work connects business priorities, technical delivery and the people responsible for putting change into practice.

How I approach transformation

I start with the operating problem: what people need to do, where the friction sits and what improvement would matter. I then connect priorities to a practical sequence, clear ownership and evidence for the next decision. Adoption belongs in that conversation from the beginning.

My approach is to create clarity, give people ownership and make progress visible. I connect the leaders setting direction with the technical and operational teams making it happen.

Languages and communication

I speak Arabic, French and English, with German at B1 level. I am based in Hamburg.
