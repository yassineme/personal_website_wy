The hierarchy I recommend.

  • Give headlines a medium-to-semibold weight, with enough space for the letterforms to stay clear.
  • Use the serif for article reading and a few expressive words.
  • Keep navigation, labels and buttons in the sans family, with clearer sizes and consistent weights.
  • Use a comfortable reading width, 20–21 px article text and generous line spacing.
